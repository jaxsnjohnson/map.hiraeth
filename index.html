Navigation & Interaction

Move freely through the map by clicking and dragging. Use your mouse wheel or trackpad to zoom in for detail or out for an overview.

  • Single Click: Identify a location or select a point for measurement.
  • Double Click: Lock the coordinate display to a specific point (useful for copying exact coordinates).
  • Click Links: Shareable links to specific Points of Interest (POIs) can be shared natively (when supported) or copied by clicking the share icon in any popup.

Toolbar Controls

  • Sidebar: Toggle the map list on the left.
  • Markers (Eye Icon): Show or hide all map markers and regions for a clear view of the terrain.
  • Filters: Open the filter panel to toggle specific categories of locations.
  • Ruler: Activate the distance measurement tool. Click multiple points to measure a path; double-click to finish.
  • Sound: Toggle ambient background audio (themed for Day/Night modes).

The World of Hiraeth

Hiraeth is a realm born of imagination, nurtured through more than a decade of shared stories and dedicated world-building by its creator and founding Dungeon Master Jax SN Johnson. Its landscapes, cities, and hidden corners have echoed with the footsteps of heroes and the schemes of villains, meticulously charted and brought to life over countless campaigns.

This interactive atlas is more than just a collection of locations; it is a living window into a world that has breathed and evolved. What began as a setting for short stories in middle school and fun campaigns in high school—full of hand-drawn sketches shared around the gaming table—has grown into this digital archive. It is born from a deep passion for this cherished setting and the collaborative magic of tabletop roleplaying.

Here, you are invited to delve into the details of bustling port cities like Rockdrift, trace the paths of ancient roads in Icebeach, or uncover the secrets whispered in forgotten ruins in Astrousia. These maps are resources designed to spark imagination, provide context, and help you visualize the stage upon which your own Hiraeth adventures will unfold.

Explore. Discover. The journey awaits.

Atlas Performance + Search Refinement

v1.2.0
  • Reworked startup loading around a generated atlas index so the map list and atlas metadata load faster without recursively fetching every map definition at boot.
  • Added on-demand full map loading, bounded idle prefetching for likely next maps, and normalized image variant support for cleaner mobile/desktop asset selection.
  • Added same-origin offline caching for the app shell, atlas data, and previously opened map assets to improve repeat visits and resilience.
  • Changed ambient sound loading to lazy-load on first use instead of eagerly downloading both tracks at startup.
  • Refined search with weighted result ranking, atlas-wide lookup, keyboard navigation, and more compact result rows with lighter visual hierarchy.
  • Polished search highlighting and result spacing so matched text no longer widens awkwardly in the search results list.

Stability + UX Polish
